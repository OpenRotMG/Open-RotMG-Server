# Game data

Runtime rules are compiled at startup from `game/123.5.1/`: `game.xml` (roots, worlds, RealmRules, landmark chests), `actors.xml` (per-object states, behaviors, loot and portal declarations), `strings.xml` (recovered StringLists) and `geometry.xml` (maps, templates, generators). Invalid or incomplete XML stops startup. Each object and layout carries `provenance`: `recovered` (Release 7.0 server XML under `reference/release-7.0/`), `corrected` (recovered with the listed corrections) or `authored` (an explicit reconstruction or native-service binding), with `origin` and `reason`. `tests/fixtures/xml_runtime/build.py` regenerates these files without editing reference bytes. Source selection follows `docs/reference-sources.md`; legacy differences and authored policies are listed in `docs/xml-migration-differences.md`.

Original research materials and the XML scripting documentation are preserved under `reference/`; see its README for provenance and scope.

Selected `objects.xml`/`grounds.xml` own identities, stats, projectiles and rendering; they and the map assets remain third-party material, and the backend license does not relicense them. `sfx/music/sorc.mp3` is the selected client's requested track, recovered unchanged from moistosaurus/realm-src `1855c017eab870d13f0b61b1cf0b9e66a01922ff`, `common/resources/web/music/sorc.mp3` (see `REALM-SRC-LICENSE`); The non-music MP3 clips in `sfx/` are unchanged files from that pin's `common/resources/web/sfx/`. No separate audio-rights grant is claimed.

- Snake Pit: seeded circular rooms, five intervening rooms, branches, filled diggable tunnels, grates, striped entrance flooring and four boss-cover walls. - Mad Lab: seeded tree of `lab-templates.json` stamps. Rooms are cut from the example layout on the Wild Shadow wiki page last updated for 123.5.1 (https://web.archive.org/web/20130508122310/http://forums.wildshadow.com/wiki/realm-mad-god/mad-lab, image `i5hVM`, a minimap at 4 px per tile): wall, floor, vat and acid shapes are tile-exact, with the stitched image's 1-tile seams cleaned; equipment walls, metal plates and the dark one-tile dots (Lab Floor Power) match the 123.5.1 minimap colours of their textures (brown in-room tiles are equipment walls, not tables); Tesla Coils are authored (recovered `dungeons.xml` places none): two stand on Lab Floor Power dots in each of the last five power rooms on the route, Dr Terrible's included; the other dots stay bare, as a destroyed coil's dot does in 2012 footage (t0f0_BWpBtw, 2012-09-08); the catwalk lattice over the acid is an estimate. MadLab Warning borders frame every open vat and acid pool and each gas spawner, but not the vat fields of the start and the L room, and catwalk covers the cluster and pair vats outside their metal frames, as in that footage and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9XOYh8DJQ8 (2012-10-09); the L room's walkway is the 2012 catwalk of plates and slat blocks, and the walkway cross's walkways, drawn on the wiki map in the same pattern, follow it; catwalk tiles lie on grounds of normal speed (Lab Bad/Good Vat No Slow, Bright Grass under the acid lattice) because the 123.5.1 client slows a player by the ground's Speed whatever stands on it. A few table rooms hold one or two destructible Lab Tables, as 2012 players remember them. Path length, branches and weights follow that map and a whole-run minimap from 2012-10-09 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=s9XOYh8DJQ8&t=300). No captured map matched: Slendergo/TKR-Source `mad_lab.jm` is a later boss arena only, Club559/ROTMGServer `madlab.wmap` uses other room sizes. Not a recovered official generator.

**Encounters, loot and portals:** enemy AI, children, transformations, loot and dungeon portals come from `actors.xml`; Rust keeps no per-kind AI or loot tables (its remaining per-kind policy tables are listed in the differences document). Loot rows and portal `MetamorphoseAtDeath` chances use recovered Release 7.0 values (for example Medusa→Abyss `.04`, Flying Brain→Mad Lab `.02`). Landmark chests in `game.xml` are authored reconstructions from the pinned realm-src comparator. `/api/drop-rates` publishes the same compiled evaluator the game uses. Release 7.0 rules are qualified era evidence, not authenticated 123.5.1 behavior.
nRealm habitat, weights, root `Spawn` groups and actor-owned children link from the selected rules. `RealmRules` records authored density, missing independent camp placements and named-site counts separately from the recovered setpiece habitats. `/api/drop-rates` exposes the Quiver restoration under `source_row.row_origin` with its base citation; the containing actor keeps its existing label.

Earned base fame is XP/1000, a reference-server policy rather than verified 123.5.1 scoring; historical death bonuses are not restored. Account-private loot and Vault access are enforced server-side.
