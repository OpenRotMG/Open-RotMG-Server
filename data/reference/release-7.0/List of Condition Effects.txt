List of Condition Effects

Players only
Quiet: Mana is reduced to 0 and remains there for the duration of the effect (i.e. no special ability).  Players only.
Weak: Attack stat is effectively set to 0.  Players only.
Sick: No hitpoint regeration.  Players only.
Blind: Player’s screen goes dark.  Players only.
Hallucinating: Textures are randomly swapped.  Players only.
Drunk: Player’s screen gets blurry.  Players only.
Confused: Player’s movement keys are remapped.  Players only.
Invisible: Can not be targeted by monsters.  Players only.
Bleeding: Slowly lose health (instead of regenerating).  Players only.
Healing: Hitpoint regeneration greatly increased.  Players only.
Speedy: Movement speed increased by 50%.  Players only.
Berserk: Attack speed increased by 50%.  Players only.
Damaging: Damage increased by 50%.  Players only.
Paused: Player can not interact with world.  Players only.
Hexed: Player is turned into a random pet. Players only.

Players and enemies
Slowed: Speed is effectively set to 0 for players or halved for monsters.
Paralyzed: Can not move.  Players or Monsters.
Armor Pierce: Projectile damage ignores defense.  Players or Enemy projectiles.
Stasis: Can not move/interact with world.  Players or Enemies.
Stasis Immune: Can not become stasis.  Players or Enemies.
Invincible: Projectiles and other damage ignore character (go through).  Players or enemies.
Invulnerable: Projectiles and other damage do not harm the creature (but will still hit).  Players or enetmies.
Armored: Defense is doubled.  Players or enemies.
Armor Broken: Defense is set to 0.  Players or enemies.
Dazed: Halves number of projectiles shot.  Enemies only.

Enemies only:
Stunned: Can not shoot.  Enemies only.
