Dungeon Template


Ground Rules


* No profane or obscene imagery or suggestions.
* No repeat entries for the same dungeon.
* All art must have a specific tag associated and listed in this document.
* Assume this dungeon will be ENDGAME. Designers will be applying health and defense values.
* This dungeon should be built in mind for multiple players; Your limit is 10+ People; If you wish to cap the number of players to 10, please address this in the section below.
* All areas of this document must be filled out unless otherwise noted. Not doing so could be detrimental to your dungeon being considered.
* Draw Tool should be used, Sprite Sheets are also accepted. 
* Follow all of the instructions for each section.
* No projectiles will currently be accepted; sorry!
* No new condition effects or behaviors: Use only examples from currently created content.
* By submission of this form you agree to the above tenants.

Group Dynamics

Mandatory: Boss Room(s)
This section should be highly detailed, diagramed, and explained. This should not be used to explain the boss, but the perils of the room, it’s shape, how players might interact with it, theming, and difficulty. Some boss information may be necessary to explain any of the aforementioned. Make a section for EACH boss room.


Diagram: 
A drawn or created diagram of the boss room -- entrance(s), where the boss is placed, any 3D objects such as walls, etc


Perils: 
What makes this room dangerous for players? What should we be attempting to recreate?


Interaction:
Can players interact with the room? How? What should be their primary concerns aside from the boss?

Aesthetics
Aesthetics cover the story and look of your dungeon. This section will be used by designers to access all art material made by you or your contributors. All applicable sections must be filled out.


Mandatory: Theme and Story
Explain in under 3 paragraphs what the story / theme of your dungeon is. Consider this the pitch.








Mandatory: Ground Tiles and Walls
In this section, give the DRAW TOOL tags specific to your dungeon’s wall and tiles. No explanation as to where they are used is needed, this will be covered later.


ex: WALLS: dwallsrichter2014    GROUND: dgroundrichter2014






Mandatory: Inanimate Objects
A MINIMUM of 3 Inanimate thematic objects are required to qualify. Examples of these are pillars, statues, etc. DRAW TOOL tags to their location is necessary.


ex: Statues: dstatuesrichter2014


Mandatory: Minion Art
All basic monster art. These are the primary minions / monsters in your dungeon. These should be CHARACTERS(8x8) if using the Draw Tool; each minion should contain a 1 standing frame, 2 frame movement animation, and 2 frame attack animation. DRAW TOOL tags to their location is necessary.


ex: Fire Mage: dfiremagerichter2014; Ice Mae: dicemagerichter2014




Mandatory: Boss Art
All Boss art. These should be CHARACTERS (16x16) if using the Draw Tool; each boss should contain a 1 standing frame, 2 frame movement animation, and 2 frame attack animation. DRAW TOOL tags to their location is necessary.


ex: Boss 1: dbossmageguyrichter2014




Optional: Complex Animations and Frames
You may want some monsters or bosses to have more complex animations or forms. If this is something you would like, we will need all of the frames, objects, and animations necessary. Instruction on when / how to use them can be explained below in their sections. Each of these sections will REQUIRE Draw Tool Tags OR Sprite Sheet.




For Special Animations: We will need EACH single frame of what you are trying to accomplish. Examples of these are special attacks that you would want to have happen that require more than the traditional 2 frame attack.


For Special Forms: If one of your minions / bosses simply changes color, or into a new sprite all together, we will need to know the object or sprite.




ex: Boss  form 1: dbossmageguyiceformrichter2014
