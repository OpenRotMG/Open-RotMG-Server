# Persona presets

`data/bot-personas.json` uses names and star-colour bands observed in public 2012 footage. Exact stars within an observed band, roles, gear, potion completion, unlock histories and vault holdings are **synthetic starting snapshots**, not reconstructions of those people's actual accounts or claims that they are playing here. Rank correlates with experience but is not proof of skill. Normal game authority takes over after spawning; absent lifetime history must remain unknown.

Showcase accounts and boss crowds are selection-biased; these observations do not establish population proportions, typical prices or reaction times.
